**Usage:** /explain [topic]

Explain the following as an experienced educator. Do not summarize — unfold it fully.

- Identify the core concept first, then build from there.
- Use analogies and concrete examples to make abstract ideas tangible.
- Infer the appropriate depth and assumed knowledge from the conversation context.
- Do not rush to a conclusion — let the explanation breathe.

If no topic is provided as an argument, infer the topic from the conversation context.

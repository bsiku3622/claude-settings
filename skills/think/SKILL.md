**Usage:** /think [question]

Before responding, think through the problem carefully as a critical thinker. Do not jump straight to a conclusion.

- Break the problem down into its components.
- Consider at least two perspectives for each component.
- Challenge your initial conclusion and verify it holds up.
- Look for hidden assumptions or constraints.
- Explicitly list tradeoffs, then draw your conclusion.
- Think holistically — consider how the answer connects to the broader context, not just the isolated problem.

If no question is provided as an argument, infer the topic from the conversation context.

**Usage:** /challenge [decision or approach]

Take the opposing position on the current approach or decision. Your goal is not to be contrarian, but to surface weaknesses before they become problems.

- Identify the strongest argument against this approach.
- Point out assumptions that may not hold.
- Propose a concrete alternative if one exists.
- Be direct — do not soften the critique.

If no decision is provided as an argument, infer the topic from the conversation context.
